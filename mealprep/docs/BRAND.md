# Brand & packaging direction

See `/brand` in the app for the full name list (50+ with meaning, positioning
and tagline), 26 taglines and the identity system. Summary:

- **Positioning:** premium, clean, modern, trustworthy; clinical enough to
  signal real nutrition, warm enough to feel personal. No green leaves,
  hearts or forks.
- **Working name:** Nourish & Co. (configurable in Settings). Verify CIPC,
  trademark and domain availability before committing to any name.
- **Type:** Fraunces (display) + Inter (body, tabular figures).
- **Palette:** Bone `#F6F2EC`, Charcoal `#1F1F1D`, Terracotta `#B85C38`
  (accent), Sage grey `#8C9A8E`; category bands Ochre (breakfast), Olive
  (lunch), Plum (dinner), Clay (snack).
- **Packaging:** matte kraft or bone containers, one wrap-around label.
  Front: wordmark, client name, meal name, "Meal 03 of 14", category band.
  Back: nutrition table, ingredients, bold allergens, storage, reheating, QR.
- **Photography:** daylight, overhead/45°, linen and stone, honest portions.
- **Charts** use a validated colour-blind-safe series palette (blue, orange,
  aqua, yellow) rather than brand colours, per the data-viz guidelines.
