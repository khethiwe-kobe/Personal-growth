What removing the gym did

Petra Nera drops R1,820 a month — R650 membership plus R1,170 in Uber fares to Planet Fitness. SoHo loses nothing in cash, but loses the benefit that was carrying it: an on-site gym is worth nothing if you do not use one. The gap moves from R380 to R2,200, and every stress test below now favours Petra Nera.

Answer: no, there is nothing better

Everything cheaper than R8,800 in this complex has one bathroom or one bedroom. The next two-bathroom unit up is the R10,500 headline one you started with, then R12,000. For a 2-bed 2-bath, R8,800 is the floor at SoHo Junction — and it is available now.

One thing to confirm

The R9,500 listing shows R10,500 as its headline but itemises R9,500 rent plus R1,400 of charges. The R8,800 listing itemises nothing. So is R8,800 the base rent, or already all-in? This model assumes base — different agency, no itemisation. If it turns out to be all-in, SoHo drops another R1,400 a month and the gap to Petra Nera closes to R800.

Questions to ask

Petra Nera
  1. Are sanitation, sewerage and refuse billed on top of the R1,150, or included? Worth up to R1,400 a month.
  2. If billed separately, what did they come to last month?
  3. Can we see a recent utility bill, so the R1,150 is confirmed rather than estimated?
  4. What does the R2,000 admin fee cover, and is it negotiable? Market rate is R950–R1,500.
  5. What is the annual escalation percentage?
  6. Security setup — guard, access control, CCTV, perimeter?
  7. How many parking bays, covered or allocated?
  8. Backup power and water storage during outages?
  9. Age and condition of geyser, plumbing and roof — who pays for repairs?
  10. Will the landlord contribute to repainting given the condition?
  11. Exact walking route to the CRC entrance — is it lit at night?
  12. When is it available, and is R9,000 held for a 12-month lease?
SoHo Junction — R8,800
  1. What is the floor size? The other unit in this complex is 62 m².
  2. What does a two-bed here actually spend on prepaid electricity a month? Ask the estate manager or a current tenant. This is the last unverified number of any size.
  3. Is the R8,800 the rent, or the all-in figure? The R9,500 unit advertises at R10,500 with the charges folded in.
  4. Confirm the levies — R900 sanitation and R500 refuse/security, or the R890.65 and R399 another SoHo listing quotes?
  5. What does prepaid water actually cost here? It is sub-metered via STSS, so it is not in your levy.
  6. Do the levies escalate annually, and by how much?
  7. Is the carport allocated, and can a second bay be rented? At what cost?
  8. What is the annual escalation percentage?
  9. Any special levies planned? Can we see the last two AGM minutes and the reserve-fund balance?
  10. Total once-off cost — admin, credit checks for two, tags, meter activation?
  11. Deposit interest arrangement, and which account?
  12. Generator or inverter for load-shedding, and water tanks for outages?
  13. Early-cancellation penalty — can it be capped in writing?
  14. Is it still available?
