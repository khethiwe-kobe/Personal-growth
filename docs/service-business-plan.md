# Side-Business Plan — Services for Young Professionals

**For:** Khethiwe · **Base:** Johannesburg · **Written:** August 2026
**Constraint that shapes everything below:** you have a full-time job. Every idea here is
judged first on **rand per hour of your time**, not on revenue.

---

## 0. The one idea behind all the ideas

Young professionals in Joburg earn decent money, have almost no time, and feel guilty about
three things: **what they eat, what they never learned, and the admin piling up.**

They will happily pay someone else to remove a recurring, dreaded, low-status task — but only
if it disappears *completely*. Half-solutions don't sell.

So the filter for you is not "what can I do?" It is:

> Can I do this **in a batch**, **on a schedule I control**, **paid upfront**, for **many
> people at once**?

Anything that fails that test (cleaning, laundry, errands-by-the-hour, dog walking) is a job,
not a business. Anything that passes it (batch cooking, group teaching, retainers, digital
products) buys your time back.

## 2. Your recommended build — three pillars + one leverage layer

```
PILLAR 1  Sunday Kitchen        meal prep      Sun (+Sat shop)     ~7 hrs/wk
PILLAR 2  Friday Water          swim blocks    Fri PM + Sat AM     ~6 hrs/wk
PILLAR 3  Sorted                admin retainer  async, from phone  ~3 hrs/wk
LAYER 4   Digital               PDFs + course   built once          0 hrs/wk
                                                            TOTAL  ~16 hrs/wk
```

They fit together deliberately:

- **Different seasons.** Swimming is a Sep–Apr business in Joburg. Meal prep is strongest
  May–Aug when nobody wants to cook in the cold. One covers the other's dip.
- **Same customer.** The 29-year-old in a Bryanston complex who buys your meals is the same
  person who can't swim and hasn't renewed their licence disc. One customer, three revenue lines.
- **Same marketing.** One Instagram account, one WhatsApp broadcast list, one referral scheme.

## PILLAR 1 — "Sunday Kitchen": premium meal prep with nutrition consultation

### Why this one first
Ready-made meal companies in Joburg are charging real money and still can't personalise. Nutridish
sells a 5-day, 2-meals-a-day plan at **R1,499** (≈R150/meal) and an "Executive" version at
**R2,099** (≈R210/meal). That is your ceiling and it is generous. You compete on the thing they
can't do: **an actual human who asked you about your goals, your training, your allergies and
your fridge.**

### Offer ladder

| Tier | What they get | Price | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|-------|
| **Taster** | 5 meals, one week, no consult | **R750** (R150/meal) | Deliberately poor value — pushes people up |
| **Core** | 10 meals/week, rotating menu | **R1,250/wk** (R125/meal) | Your volume tier |
| **Premium** | 10 meals/wk + macro targets + monthly 30-min check-in | **R1,500/wk** (R150/meal) | Your profit tier |
| **Onboarding consult** | 45 min: goals, allergies, training load, likes/hates, written plan | **R650 once-off** | Waived on 4-week Premium prepay |
| **4-week prepay** | Any tier × 4, upfront | **8% off** | Cash upfront, no weekly chasing |
| **Add-ons** | Extra protein portion R45 · breakfast jars R55 · snack pack R120/wk | | Pure margin |

**Delivery:** do **not** drive. Two collection windows (Sun 17:00–19:00) plus a courier option
(Droppa/Pingo, ~R60–R90 a drop in Joburg) charged at a flat **R75**. Radius cap: 12 km.

### Unit economics (per meal)

| Line | Cost |
|------|------|
| Protein (180 g chicken / lean mince) | R25 |
| Starch (rice, sweet potato, pasta) | R6 |
| Vegetables | R12 |
| Oil, spice, sauce base | R4 |
| Container + lid | R6 |
| Label | R1 |
| **COGS per meal** | **≈ R54** |

Premium proteins (salmon, beef fillet, lamb) push COGS to ~R85 — price those meals at R185 or
offer them as a R45 add-on. Never absorb them.

| Tier | Price | COGS | Gross profit | Margin |
|------|-------|------|--------------|--------|
| Core | R125 | R54 | **R71** | 57% |
| Premium | R150 | R54 | **R96** | 64% |
| Taster | R150 | R54 | R96 | 64% |

### Monthly P&L at three scales

Assume 4 cook weeks a month.

| | **Starter** (4 clients) | **Target** (6 clients) | **Stretch** (10 clients) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meals/week | 40 | 60 | 100 |
| Mix | 2 Core, 2 Premium | 3 Core, 3 Premium | 5 Core, 5 Premium |
| **Revenue/month** | **R22,000** | **R33,000** | **R55,000** |
| COGS | −R8,640 | −R12,960 | −R21,600 |
| Gas + electricity | −R400 | −R500 | −R700 |
| Marketing | −R500 | −R500 | −R800 |
| Insurance + admin + fees | −R450 | −R550 | −R750 |
| Helper (R450/day × 4) | — | — | −R1,800 |
| **Net profit/month** | **≈ R12,010** | **≈ R18,490** | **≈ R29,350** |
| Your hours/month | 26 | 30 | 44 |
| **Rand per hour** | **R462** | **R616** | **R667** |

Consultations sit on top: 6 new clients a quarter × R650 = **+R3,900 per quarter**.

**Your fixed costs are tiny (~R1,400/month), so you break even on your second client.** At 3 Core
clients you're netting about **R7,100 a month** — which pays back the entire lean startup cost by
month 3. The business is never underwater; it's just small until you fill Sunday.

### Your actual Sunday

| Time | Task |
|------|------|
| Sat 09:00–11:00 | Shop (one Makro/Food Lover's run, single list for all clients) |
| Sun 08:00–09:00 | Mise en place — all veg cut, all protein portioned |
| Sun 09:00–11:30 | Cook in three batches (oven trays + two big pans running in parallel) |
| Sun 11:30–12:30 | Portion on a scale, label, seal, blast-chill |
| Sun 12:30–13:00 | Clean down |
| Sun 17:00–19:00 | Collection window (client comes to you) |
| **Total** | **≈ 7 hrs** |

The whole thing lives or dies on **menu discipline**: **one menu per week for everyone**, with
three swap slots (protein, starch, no-go ingredient). Custom menus per client will destroy you.

### Legal, non-negotiable
- **Certificate of Acceptability (CoA)** for the premises under R638 of 2018 — apply to your
  local municipality's Environmental Health Practitioner. Free or nominal; an inspector visits.
  Trading without it is the single biggest risk in this plan.
- **Food handler hygiene training** — ~R900.
- Fridge/freezer thermometer log, and a signed allergy declaration from every client.
- Register as a sole proprietor for tax; the CIPC company (~R175) can wait until you're over
  ~R20k/month.

## PILLAR 2 — "Friday Water": swim blocks for adults and kids

### Why the market is genuinely enormous
The NSRI puts the share of South Africans who can swim at roughly **15%**. Nearly every adult
class in the country is oversubscribed, and the buyer — an embarrassed 30-year-old professional —
has both the money and a reason to keep it discreet. Nobody is marketing to them properly.

Grounding on what Joburg already charges:
- Superprof's Joburg average: **R430** a lesson.
- Sandton Seals: **R800/month** for one lesson a week (≈R200/lesson).
- Pool Academy: private **R700/month** for 4 × 30 min; **R925/month** at your residence.
- Sarah's Swim Academy: an 8-day course at **R3,750**, max 5 swimmers.

**Private lessons are a trap.** R350 for 30 minutes sounds great until you realise it caps you
at R700/hour and every cancellation is dead time. **Small groups, sold as prepaid blocks,** is
where the money is.

### Offer

| Product | Format | Price/person | Per hour to you |
|---------|--------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Adult Confidence Block** | 6 × 45 min, max 4 people | **R1,680** (R280/session) | **R1,120** (full group) |
| **Kids Squad Block** | 6 × 30 min, max 6 kids | **R1,080** (R180/session) | **R1,080** (full group) |
| **Private adult** | 30 min | **R350** | R700 |
| **Holiday intensive** | 5 days × 45 min, max 4 | **R2,200** | R1,170 |
| Assessment session | 20 min, credited against a block | R150 | — |

Sold as **prepaid 6-week blocks only**. No pay-per-lesson. This kills no-shows, gives you cash
upfront, and means you do the admin once every six weeks instead of every Friday.

### Weekly timetable (5 contact hours)

| Slot | Class | Capacity |
|------|-------|----------|
| Fri 16:00 | Kids Squad | 6 |
| Fri 17:00 | Adult Confidence | 4 |
| Sat 08:00 | Adult Confidence | 4 |
| Sat 09:00 | Kids Squad | 6 |
| Sat 10:00 | Adult Confidence | 4 |

### Economics per 6-week cycle

| | Conservative (60% full) | Full |
|---|---|---|
| Adult places sold (12 available) | 7 | 12 |
| Kids places sold (12 available) | 8 | 12 |
| Adult revenue | R11,760 | R20,160 |
| Kids revenue | R8,640 | R12,960 |
| **Revenue per cycle** | **R20,400** | **R33,120** |
| Pool hire (R300/hr × 5 × 6 wks) | −R9,000 | −R9,000 |
| Public liability insurance | −R900 | −R900 |
| Marketing | −R600 | −R600 |
| **Net per cycle** | **≈ R9,900** | **≈ R22,620** |
| **Net per month** (6 wks = 1.5 months) | **≈ R6,600** | **≈ R15,080** |
| Your hours/month | 26 | 26 |
| **Rand per hour** | **R254** | **R580** |

**Pool hire is the whole game.** R300/hour is an assumption — go get three real quotes before
you commit:
1. A homeowner with a heated pool in a quiet complex (cheapest, most flexible, R200–R350/hr).
2. A school pool on weekends (R250–R500/hr, often keen for off-season income).
3. A guest house or lodge (they love the weekday-afternoon utilisation).

Negotiate a **fixed 6-week block rate** and you can drop this to R250/hr. If you can find a
partner who takes 20% of revenue instead of a fixed fee, take it — it removes your downside
entirely in a slow cycle.

### Non-negotiable before you take a cent
- **Swimming SA / Learn-to-Swim instructor qualification** — budget R3,000–R5,000.
- **Valid first aid (Level 1) + CPR** — ~R900, renew every 2–3 years.
- **Public liability insurance** — R350–R600/month. Teaching adults to swim without this is
  reckless; one incident ends the business and more.
- Signed indemnity + medical disclosure from every client, and a hard 4-person adult cap.

### The seasonality problem — plan for it now
Joburg pools are unusable roughly **May–August**. Two options:
1. **Accept it.** Run 3 cycles Sep–Apr (~R30k–R50k net), and push meal prep hard in winter — that's
   exactly when meal prep demand peaks. This is the simpler plan.
2. **Find an indoor heated pool** (Virgin Active partner clubs, private schools, some estates).
   Hire is 30–50% higher, so raise the adult block to R1,980 and market it as "learn before summer,
   arrive at December already swimming." That angle sells surprisingly well in June.

## PILLAR 3 — "Sorted": the life-admin retainer

Almost no cost, no premises, no stock, done from your phone between meetings. This is your
highest rand-per-hour line and the one people underestimate.

### What you actually do
Vehicle licence disc renewals · TV licence · Home Affairs appointment bookings · medical aid
claim submissions and follow-ups · insurance quote comparisons · booking the plumber / car
service / dentist · cancelling subscriptions and negotiating contracts · gift buying and delivery ·
restaurant and travel bookings · chasing a refund · gathering documents for a bond application.

### Pricing

| Product | Price | Your time |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| Single task (up to 30 min) | **R250** | 30 min |
| **Life Admin Power Hour** — 60-min video call, you do their bookings live while they watch | **R750** | 60 min |
| **Sorted Lite** — 3 tasks/month | **R650/month** | ~1.5 hrs |
| **Sorted Plus** — 8 tasks/month + WhatsApp line | **R1,500/month** | ~4 hrs |

Client pays every third-party fee upfront into your account before you act. You never float
their money. Petrol/courier billed at cost + R50.

### Realistic month

| | Clients | Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Sorted Lite | 6 | R3,900 |
| Sorted Plus | 3 | R4,500 |
| Power Hours | 2 | R1,500 |
| **Total** | | **R9,900** |

### One hard boundary

## 4. The 12-month plan

### Month 1–2 — Foundation (income R0–R6,000)
- [ ] Apply for the CoA. Start this week; the inspection is the long pole.
- [ ] Book food handler training and, if you're doing swim, the SwimSA course.
- [ ] Get **three real pool-hire quotes**. Do not build the swim model on my R300/hr guess.
- [ ] Build a one-page site: offers, prices, a "book a consult" form, Payfast. One evening's work.
- [ ] Recruit **3 pilot meal clients at 50%** in exchange for a written testimonial and photos.
- [ ] Cook 4 test Sundays. Time yourself. Find out what your real capacity is before you sell it.

### Month 3–4 — Sell the first pillar properly (target R18,000–R22,000/mo)
- [ ] Fill to 6 meal-prep clients. Price at full rate now — the pilots move up or move on.
- [ ] Launch the first 6-week swim cycle (Sep, if you're following the season).
- [ ] Open Sorted quietly to your existing network: 3 retainer clients is enough to start.
- [ ] Set up a debit order or Payfast subscription. Chasing weekly payments will burn you out
      faster than the cooking will.

### Month 5–7 — Add leverage (target R30,000–R38,000/mo)
- [ ] Pitch **two office managers** in Sandton/Rosebank on the corporate lunchbox drop. One office
      of 15 people, twice a week, is R16,200/month from a single delivery stop — better than any
      six individual clients.
- [ ] Run swim cycle 2, filled from cycle-1 referrals.
- [ ] Ship the R249 meal plan PDF.
- [ ] Hire a helper for Sundays at R450/day the week you cross 80 meals.

### Month 8–12 — Consolidate (target R45,000–R55,000/mo)
- [ ] Second corporate account.
- [ ] Ship the swim video course.
- [ ] Raise prices ~10% for new clients only; grandfather everyone loyal.
- [ ] **Drop the weakest line.** By month 12 you will know which of the three you actually enjoy.
      Kill one on purpose rather than letting all three go mediocre.

## 5. Getting clients (in priority order)

1. **Estate and complex WhatsApp groups.** By far the highest-ROI channel in Joburg. Your buyer
   lives in Bryanston, Fourways, Greenside, Melrose Arch. One good post in a 300-home group
   beats R5,000 of Instagram spend. Get a resident to post it for you — third-party
   recommendations convert 5–10× better than self-promotion.
2. **Gyms.** Leave cards at a CrossFit box, F45 or Virgin Active. Their members are already
   spending money on their bodies and already failing at nutrition. Offer the trainer a R150
   referral fee.
3. **Corporates.** Email two office managers a week. Script: "I deliver 15 chef-prepared,
   macro-balanced lunches to one reception desk twice a week. No admin for you, staff pay me
   directly. Can I do a free tasting for your team?" Free tastings close these.
4. **Referral scheme.** R150 account credit per referral, both sides. Automate it in the sign-up
   form so you never have to remember.
5. **Instagram, but batched.** Film four Sunday cook reels in one session, schedule them across
   the month. Do not let content become a second job.
6. **Swim specifically:** post in adult beginner groups, target ages 25–40, and lead with the
   emotional line — *"You're 32 and you can't swim. Nobody has to know but us."* That sentence
   sells better than any feature list.

## 6. Rules that protect your time

Write these on the wall.

1. **One menu a week.** Three swap slots, no bespoke menus below the Premium tier.
2. **Prepaid, always.** Blocks and subscriptions only. No pay-as-you-go, no cash on collection.
3. **Collection first, delivery as a paid extra.** Never build a driving route into your week.
4. **12 km radius.** Hard stop.
5. **Two service lines active at once, maximum**, until one is genuinely on autopilot.
6. **Batch the admin.** One 90-minute block on a Wednesday evening: invoices, menus, orders,
   messages. Not continuously, all week.
7. **Book capacity, not clients.** When Sunday is full, it's full. A waiting list is an asset —
   it's also how you justify your next price rise.
8. **Every new idea must beat R400/hour**, or it doesn't get added.

## 7. Risks, honestly

| Risk | Severity | What you do about it |
|------|----------|---------------------|
| No CoA / food safety incident | **Critical** | Get the CoA before your first paid meal. Temperature logs. Allergy declarations signed. |
| Swim incident without insurance/qualification | **Critical** | Qualification + public liability + indemnity forms before the first lesson. No exceptions. |
| Pool hire costs more than R300/hr | High | Get quotes in month 1. Model breaks at ~R550/hr; renegotiate or go revenue-share. |
| Winter kills swim income | High | Planned for — meal prep peaks in exactly those months. |
| Client churn | Medium | Prepaid blocks and 4-week prepay discounts make churn visible a month early. |
| You burn out | **Medium-high, and most likely** | The 16-hour cap is the plan, not an aspiration. Hire the Sunday helper *before* you need them. |
| Load shedding mid-cook | Medium | Gas burner backup, and cook in the morning window. |
| Solo-operator ceiling | Medium | This is why Layer 4 exists. Digital and corporate scale without more of your hours. |

## 8. If you only do one thing

Start **meal prep with 3 pilot clients this month**, while the CoA application processes and the
swim qualification is booked. It has the shortest path to cash, the clearest costs, and it will
teach you your real capacity — which is the number every other decision in this document depends on.

Then add swimming in September, when the water warms up and every parent in Joburg suddenly
remembers their child can't swim.
