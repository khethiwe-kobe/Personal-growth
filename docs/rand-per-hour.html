Working assumptions

Sunday Kitchen

One menu a week for everyone, three swap slots, collection rather than delivery. Break-even is — — below that the fixed costs eat you.

Corporate lunchbox drop

The highest-leverage line in the business: many meals, one delivery stop, one invoice. Two office managers a week is the entire sales effort.

Friday Water

Prepaid blocks to small groups, never pay-as-you-go privates. A slot is one hour of pool time whatever the lesson length — that is what you are charged for. Pool hire is the assumption to go and verify first: raise it to R550 and watch the line collapse.

Sorted

Licence discs, Home Affairs bookings, medical aid claims, insurance quotes. No stock, no premises, done from your phone. The client pays every third-party fee upfront — you never float their money.

Built once, sold forever

The only line with no hours attached — which makes it the only one that grows without costing you a weekend. You already ship software; most of your competition cannot.

The rules that protect your time

  1. 1One menu a week. Three swap slots. No bespoke menus below Premium.
  2. 2Prepaid blocks and subscriptions only. No pay-as-you-go, no cash on collection.
  3. 3Collection first. Delivery is a paid extra that somebody else drives.
  4. 4Twelve kilometre radius. Hard stop.
  5. 5Two lines active at once, maximum, until one is genuinely on autopilot.
  6. 6Admin in one 90-minute block on a Wednesday. Not continuously, all week.
  7. 7Book capacity, not clients. A waiting list is how you justify the next price rise.
  8. 8Nothing new gets added unless it beats R400 an hour.

Before your first paying client

  • Certificate of Acceptability from your municipal health inspector. Longest lead time — start it this week.
  • Food handler hygiene training, and a signed allergy declaration from every meal client.
  • Swim instructor qualification, valid first aid, and public liability cover before anyone enters the water.
  • Three real pool-hire quotes. Every swim figure here rests on a guess until you have them.
  • No filing anyone's tax return for a fee — gathering their documents is fine, submitting is not.
