The Rhythm
What the research says makes a vision actually happen
Habakkuk 2:2–3 · NLT
“Write my answer plainly on tablets, so that a runner can carry the correct message to others. If it seems slow in coming, wait patiently, for it will surely take place.”
Six findings this board is built on
  • Write it, don’t wish it. Matthews, Dominican University — goals written, action commitments written, weekly progress sent to a friend: ~76% achieved vs 43%.
  • Picture the process, not the prize. Pham & Taylor, UCLA — rehearsing the studying beat picturing the grade.
  • Contrast the wish with the obstacle. Oettingen’s WOOP — fantasy alone predicts less achievement.
  • “If–then” beats intention. Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 94 studies — naming when and where you act.
  • Specific and hard beats “do your best.” Locke & Latham — vague goals produce vague effort.
  • Look daily. A goal you see is a goal your mind keeps solving in the background.
The cadence
  • Daily · 60 seconds. Read the headline out loud. Name one thing today that serves it.
  • Weekly · Sunday 18:00. Ten minutes at the board — then send it to one person who will ask about it.
  • Monthly. The numbers: R16 000 moved, gym sessions, chapters read, membership.
  • Quarterly. Re-read Sheet C5. Adjust the plan, never the purpose.
